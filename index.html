MoveMonkey

久坐提醒，适时起身。

MoveMonkey 是一款适用于 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 的久坐提醒 App。久坐达到设定时长时，MoveMonkey 会准时提醒你起身活动；完成一分钟活动后，App 会给出庆祝反馈。

A sedentary reminder for iPhone and Apple Watch. When you have been sitting for the time you set, it reminds you to get up; after a minute of activity, it celebrates with you.
