Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

MoveMonkey is a stand-up reminder app for iPhone and Apple Watch, developed and operated by An Donghao, an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains what personal information MoveMonkey processes, why and how it’s processed, how long it’s kept, where it’s processed, and how you can exercise your rights. Please read it carefully before you use MoveMonkey.

Highlights

  • You don’t need an account to use MoveMonkey, and we don’t collect your name, email address, or phone number; we receive your email address only when you email us.
  • Stand and step data is processed only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and is never uploaded. The one exception is the log you can choose to attach on the Send Feedback screen: once you send that email, the stand and step data in the log reaches us with it.
  • So that MoveMonkey can remind you on time even while it’s in the background, our server keeps a push token and reminder times (worked out from when you last got up, with no Health data), along with a small amount of information for statistics and troubleshooting. What each item is for and how long it’s kept are listed in Appendix 1.
  • MoveMonkey contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, or third-party tracking code, and we don’t sell personal information.
  • If you downloaded MoveMonkey from the China mainland App Store, it connects to our server only after you agree to this policy, and the server is in Beijing. If you downloaded it from the App Store in another country or region, the server is in Singapore.
  • After you delete MoveMonkey from a device, that device’s records on our server are deleted automatically within 30 days. If you downloaded MoveMonkey from the China mainland App Store, the server also keeps its access log for 6 months, as required by law.

1. Who we are

Your personal information is handled by An Donghao, an independent developer. Contact: support@movemonkey.app.

You don’t need an account to use MoveMonkey. Its basic feature is to remind you to get up, based on the hours you set in Reminder Hours and how long you choose to sit before a reminder (Remind After). Using the basic feature requires no personal information.

2. Why MoveMonkey needs a server

iOS doesn’t let MoveMonkey keep running in the background. To remind you on time, our server keeps a push token (a random code that Apple assigns to each installation of MoveMonkey; it contains no name, Apple Account, or device information) and your upcoming reminder times. When a reminder time arrives, the server wakes MoveMonkey through the Apple Push Notification service, and MoveMonkey reads the data on your device, then decides whether to remind you.

The server doesn’t receive steps, stand minutes, or other Health data, and has no way of knowing your name or Apple Account. However, because reminder times are worked out from when you last got up, they roughly reveal that time.

The server sends iPhone a visible notification in only two cases: when you’re wearing Apple Watch and it decides on the watch itself that it’s time for you to get up, it reminds you and also asks the server to send iPhone the same reminder; and when Apple Watch has been taken off your wrist and asks iPhone to take over reminders, but iPhone doesn’t respond, the server sends that iPhone a notification asking you to open the app. Apart from these, everything the server sends is an invisible background push.

Each item the server keeps, what it’s for, and how long it’s kept are listed in Appendix 1.

3. Health data

With your permission, MoveMonkey reads stand minutes, stand hours, and steps from the Health app, and, through the Motion & Fitness permission, steps from the motion sensors of your iPhone and Apple Watch; while you’re up and moving, Apple Watch briefly starts a workout to read its sensor data in real time. This data is used to tell whether you’ve been sitting too long, decide when to remind you, tell whether you’ve been up and moving, and count your stand hours.

This data is processed only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and is never uploaded. The times you got up, worked out from it, are kept only on your devices and deleted when you delete MoveMonkey (for the times you got up using Just Moved or Get Up, see section 4). When Save as Workout is off, the workout is discarded as soon as it ends; when it’s on, it’s saved to the Health app as an Indoor Walk workout, which you manage in the Health app. The banana award appears only after you reach your Daily Stand Goal, so the number of times you tap it (see section 5) indirectly shows that you’ve reached your goal, though the count includes neither your stand hours nor the dates you reached the goal.

Sensitive personal information. Stand and step data is health and physiological information, and is sensitive personal information. Processing it is necessary because telling whether you’ve been sitting too long, and postponing a reminder after you get up, is the core purpose of MoveMonkey. It’s processed only on your devices; it’s never uploaded, sold, or provided to anyone, and never used for advertising or marketing. The one exception is the log you can choose to attach to an email on the Send Feedback screen: once you send that email, the stand and step data in the log reaches us with it (see section 14). If you don’t allow it to be read, you can still use the basic feature; reminders just aren’t postponed when you get up.

4. Reminder settings, the times you got up, and iCloud

Your reminder settings in MoveMonkey (such as Reminder Hours, Remind After, Daily Stand Goal, and Lunch Break) and the times you got up are kept on your devices and synced between your iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity.

When you’re signed in to iCloud, your iPhone also stores your reminder settings, and the times you got up using Just Moved or Get Up (only when, how, and on which device each was recorded, with no steps, stand minutes, or other Health data), in your own iCloud (Apple’s CloudKit private database), so they come back when you reinstall MoveMonkey or move to a new iPhone. Apple processes this information under its privacy policy; we can’t read it, and it doesn’t pass through our server on its way to iCloud.

Information on your devices is deleted when you delete MoveMonkey; information in iCloud is kept until you delete it (to delete it, see section 8).

5. Usage statistics

To check that the service is working properly, we use each device’s records on the server and the server operating log to compile aggregate figures, such as the share of pushes that arrive on time and the number of devices and people using MoveMonkey. These figures contain no information about any single device or person. The access log and the error log aren’t used for these figures.

Your iPhone also sends MoveMonkey’s version number, the state of its notifications and Background App Refresh, some of your reminder settings, and how many times some features are used and screenshots are taken. These are used to count devices by version and the share that can’t receive reminders, to learn whether reminders are useful, whether these features get used, and whether the default settings suit people, and to troubleshoot problems; none of them is Health data. If you downloaded MoveMonkey from an App Store outside China mainland, your iPhone also sends the country or region associated with your Apple Account, the language MoveMonkey is displayed in, and the number of times you tap the banana award, and your iPhone and Apple Watch both send two device link codes. These are used to count iPhones by country or region and by language, to calculate the number of people using MoveMonkey and how many of them use Apple Watch, to learn whether the reward for reaching the goal is popular, and to troubleshoot problems. The banana award appears only after you reach your Daily Stand Goal, so the number of times you tap it indirectly shows that you’ve reached your goal. The device link codes can’t be turned back into the codes they came from; we can’t use them to identify your Apple Account, and we won’t try to use them to identify who you are. For details, see Appendix 1.

6. Where the server is located, and third parties

  • Our server: If you downloaded MoveMonkey from the China mainland App Store, the server is in Beijing, China (see section 13). If you downloaded it from the App Store in another country or region, the server is in Singapore, hosted on our behalf by Alibaba Cloud (Singapore) Private Limited. All communication between your devices and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Apple Push Notification service: When a reminder time arrives, our server sends a push to your device through the Apple Push Notification service. To do this, the server gives Apple the push token; the push contains the wake type (reminder, retry, check, wake, take over, or Get Up) and the time it was sent; a reminder the server sends to iPhone also contains the reminder’s text (which shows how long you’ve been sitting) and when Apple Watch started reminding you. Pushes contain no other personal information. The servers of the Apple Push Notification service may be located outside China mainland, and Apple processes this information under its privacy policy.

Entrusted processing. The server is hosted by Alibaba Cloud (China mainland: Alibaba Cloud Computing Co., Ltd.; other countries or regions: Alibaba Cloud (Singapore) Private Limited). We have entrusted it with providing servers and storage, have agreed with it that it processes information only on our instructions, and supervise it.

MoveMonkey contains no third-party SDKs, and we don’t sell personal information. For the personal information shared with third parties, see Appendix 2.

7. What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account, and we don’t collect your name, email address, or phone number. When you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply to you, troubleshoot problems, and improve MoveMonkey.
  • We don’t request location permission.
  • We include no advertising or third-party tracking code, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

8. Your choices and rights

You have the right to know about, decide on, access, copy, correct, and delete your personal information, to withdraw your consent, and to ask for an explanation.

  • Information on your devices: You can view and change your reminder settings in MoveMonkey. Deleting MoveMonkey deletes all of its data on your devices and stops it from sending information to the server.
  • Information in iCloud: Reminder settings and the times you got up that are stored in your iCloud aren’t deleted when you delete MoveMonkey. To delete them, open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud. Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage, tap MoveMonkey, then tap Delete Data from iCloud.
  • Stand and step data: Open the Health app, tap Summary, then tap your profile picture or initials. Under Privacy, tap Apps, tap MoveMonkey, then turn off the relevant categories. If you downloaded MoveMonkey from the China mainland App Store, you can also turn off Stand and Step Data in MoveMonkey (see section 13).
  • Information on the server: After you delete MoveMonkey, its records are deleted automatically within 30 days. If you downloaded MoveMonkey from the China mainland App Store, you can also turn off On-Time Reminders, and its records are then deleted within 30 days as well (see section 13). Records on the server are identified by push token and don’t correspond to any name, and we can’t use the device link codes in them to identify your Apple Account, so we can’t look up a record by your name or email address. Please delete them yourself in one of the ways above.
  • Other requests: Send an email to support@movemonkey.app, and we’ll reply within 15 business days.
  • Withdrawing your consent doesn’t affect processing carried out on the basis of your consent before you withdrew it.
  • If you downloaded MoveMonkey from an App Store outside China mainland and believe your rights in your personal information have been infringed, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live. If you downloaded it from the China mainland App Store, see section 13.

9. Children

MoveMonkey is rated 9+, but it isn’t designed specifically for children, and it doesn’t collect any additional information based on age. Children under 14 should use MoveMonkey only after a parent or other guardian has read this policy and agreed to it; the guardian can withdraw consent at any time as described in section 8, or delete MoveMonkey.

10. Information security

All communication between your devices and the server is encrypted. Only we can access the server, and it keeps only the information listed in Appendix 1. If a security incident such as a personal information leak occurs, we’ll tell you through MoveMonkey or on this page, and report it as required.

11. Diagnostics provided by Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous diagnostics for MoveMonkey, such as crash reports. Apple processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can turn this off at any time: go to the Settings app, tap Privacy & Security, tap Analytics & Improvements, then turn off Share With App Developers.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, we’ll post the update on this page and change the effective date above.

13. China mainland

If you downloaded MoveMonkey from the China mainland App Store, this section applies to you.

The first time you open MoveMonkey, it asks you to read this policy and choose whether to agree. Until you choose, MoveMonkey requests no permissions, sends no information to our server, and stores nothing in your iCloud.

  • If you agree to this policy: MoveMonkey sends its push token, reminder times, and related information to our server (see section 2), so that it can remind you on time even while it’s in the background. It also sends the version number, the state of notifications and Background App Refresh, some of your reminder settings, and how many times some features are used and screenshots are taken (see section 5), for statistics, troubleshooting, and improving MoveMonkey. Your reminder settings and some of the times you got up are also stored in your own iCloud (see section 4).
  • Stand and step data: After you agree to this policy, MoveMonkey asks for your consent to this separately. If you don’t agree, reminders arrive at their scheduled times and aren’t postponed when you get up; Get Up only runs a timer, and when it ends, it asks whether you got up.
  • If you don’t agree to this policy: You can still use the basic feature. Reminders arrive at their scheduled times, and MoveMonkey doesn’t read Health data, connect to the server, or store anything in iCloud. Reminders may sometimes be late, and may still arrive after you’ve gotten up.

You can turn Stand and Step Data and On-Time Reminders on or off at any time: in MoveMonkey’s Settings (the gear at the top left of its main screen), tap About. When you turn off On-Time Reminders or withdraw your consent, the server stops sending pushes to your iPhone and Apple Watch and deletes their reminder times and everything else in their records, keeping only each device’s push token, whether that token belongs to iPhone or Apple Watch, when the pushes stopped, and a mark that they’ve stopped. These are used only to refuse requests later sent on that device’s behalf. They’re deleted when the device sends information again, or automatically after 30 days.

The server is in Beijing, China, hosted on our behalf by Alibaba Cloud Computing Co., Ltd. All information MoveMonkey sends to the server is stored and processed within China mainland. As required by law, this server keeps an access log for 6 months (see Appendix 1). MoveMonkey doesn’t send the country or region, the language, the number of times you tap the banana award, or the device link codes described in section 5.

iCloud in China mainland is operated by GCBD (AIPO Cloud (Guizhou) Technology Co. Ltd).

If the purposes or methods of processing, or the types of personal information processed, change, we’ll ask for your consent again the next time you open MoveMonkey.

If you believe your rights in your personal information have been infringed, you can file a complaint or report with the cyberspace affairs authorities or another authority responsible for personal information protection.

14. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, send an email to support@movemonkey.app.

You can also write to us from MoveMonkey: in its Settings (the gear at the top left of its main screen), tap About, then tap Send Feedback to write an email in the Mail app. The email is filled in with MoveMonkey’s version and the models and system versions of your iPhone and Apple Watch, which you can edit or delete before sending. If you choose, MoveMonkey’s log can be attached too; it includes recent reminder times, times you got up, stand minutes, and steps, and you can view it before attaching it. This information reaches us only after you send the email, is used only to reply to you, troubleshoot problems, and improve MoveMonkey, and is deleted within 30 days after we’ve finished handling your email.

Two codes of 16 letters and digits each. One is derived from a random install code MoveMonkey creates on iPhone when it’s first opened, and is the same on an iPhone and the Apple Watch paired with it. The other is derived from the account code iCloud assigns to MoveMonkey (unique to this app; other apps get a different one), and is the same on every iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Neither can be turned back into the code it came from, neither contains a name, email address, or phone number, and we can’t use them to identify your Apple Account
Source
Calculated on iPhone and sent with its requests; iPhone also passes them to its paired Apple Watch, which sends them with its own (only for MoveMonkey downloaded from an App Store outside China mainland; the second code is sent only while iPhone is signed in to iCloud and iCloud isn’t turned off for MoveMonkey)
Purpose
To tell which iPhones and Apple Watches belong to the same person, so we can compile aggregate figures such as the number of people using MoveMonkey and how many of them use Apple Watch, and to troubleshoot problems; not used for any other purpose. We won’t try to use them to identify who you are
Where it’s processed
Singapore
How long it’s kept
Only the most recently received codes are kept, and they’re deleted along with that device’s records. The first 8 characters of each are written to the server operating log when the codes are first received, when they change, and when that device’s records are deleted automatically because Apple reports that the push token is no longer valid or because 30 days pass without any information from that device. The log keeps them for the full 30 days, even if that device’s records are deleted sooner

Access log

Information
The time, IP address, path, and result of each access (not including push tokens or what was sent)
Source
Recorded by the Beijing server (for MoveMonkey downloaded from the China mainland App Store and for visits to the movemonkey.cn website)
Purpose
To keep network logs as required by the Cybersecurity Law of the People’s Republic of China and other laws and regulations, and to provide them when the relevant authorities make a lawful inquiry; not used for any other purpose
Where it’s processed
Beijing, China
How long it’s kept
6 months, then deleted automatically. The Singapore server keeps no access log

Feedback emails

Information
Your email address and what you write; MoveMonkey’s version and the models and system versions of your iPhone and Apple Watch, filled into the email (you can edit or delete them before sending); and, if you choose to attach it, MoveMonkey’s log (which includes recent reminder times, times you got up, stand minutes, and steps)
Source
Sent by you from your email app after you tap Send Feedback on MoveMonkey’s About screen
Purpose
To reply to you, troubleshoot problems, and improve MoveMonkey; not used for any other purpose
How long it’s kept
Deleted within 30 days after we’ve finished handling the email

Appendix 2: List of personal information shared with third parties

Apple Inc. (Apple Push Notification service)

Information shared
The push token, and the push content: the wake type and the time it was sent; a reminder also contains its text (which shows how long you’ve been sitting) and when Apple Watch started reminding you
Purpose
To send pushes to your device
Method
The server calls the Apple Push Notification service API
Contact
Apple privacy inquiries: apple.com/legal/privacy/contact

Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

Information shared
When you open a page on the movemonkey.app website in a browser, your browser sends Cloudflare your IP address and browser information, along with the page’s address, the page you came from, and how long the page took to load. The MoveMonkey app (including the privacy policy inside it) and the movemonkey.cn website don’t use Cloudflare Web Analytics
Purpose
To count visits to each page of the website and see how quickly pages load. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies, and we see only aggregate figures
Method
Your browser runs Cloudflare’s analytics script on the website’s pages and sends the information directly to Cloudflare
Contact
Cloudflare Privacy Policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
