常见问题

提醒未按时送达
请确认已在“设置” App 中允许 MoveMonkey 的通知、“健康” App 的读取权限和“运动与健身”权限，且未在 App 切换器中关闭 MoveMonkey。App 被关闭后，系统不会再将其唤醒；重新打开 MoveMonkey 即可恢复提醒。

专注模式打开时 Apple Watch 不震动
工作、个人等专注模式打开时，Apple Watch 只有在该专注模式允许 MoveMonkey 时才会震动；专注模式允许“即时通知”时，久坐提醒仍会在 iPhone 上响起。要让 Apple Watch 也震动：

  1. 前往“设置” App，轻点“专注模式”，然后选取一个专注模式。
  2. 轻点“App”，然后轻点“允许下列通知”。
  3. 轻点 ＋，然后选择 MoveMonkey。

工作、个人等你坐着时常用的专注模式，每个都需要添加一次。添加后，可在 MoveMonkey 的“设置”中轻点“发送测试提醒”确认。

如果该专注模式使用的是“静音下列通知”，请确认 MoveMonkey 不在其中。如果 iPhone 也不响，请在同一页打开“即时通知”，再前往“设置” App，轻点“通知”，轻点 MoveMonkey，然后打开“即时通知”。

如果希望某个专注模式打开时完全不提醒（比如睡眠），可在该模式的“专注模式过滤条件”中添加 MoveMonkey，并打开“暂停久坐提醒”。

是否需要 Apple Watch
不需要。仅使用 iPhone 即可接收提醒；佩戴 Apple Watch 时，站立判断更准确，提醒也会显示在手腕上。

健康数据是否会上传
不会。所有判断均在你的设备上完成；服务器收到的是据此算出的提醒时间，不是健康数据，详见隐私政策。

Questions

Reminders arrive late
Check that MoveMonkey is allowed notifications, Health access and Motion & Fitness access in Settings, and that it has not been closed in the App Switcher. The system does not wake an app that has been closed; open MoveMonkey again to resume reminders.

The Apple Watch doesn’t tap while a Focus is on
While a Focus such as Work or Personal is on, the Apple Watch taps only if that Focus allows MoveMonkey; the iPhone still sounds the reminder when the Focus allows Time Sensitive Notifications. To have the Apple Watch tap too:

  1. Go to Settings, tap Focus, then choose a Focus.
  2. Tap Apps, then tap Allow Notifications From.
  3. Tap +, then select MoveMonkey.

Add it once to each Focus you use while sitting, such as Work or Personal. Then tap Send Test Reminder (发送测试提醒) in MoveMonkey’s settings to check.

If the Focus uses Silence Notifications From, make sure MoveMonkey is not in it. If the iPhone is silent as well, turn on Time Sensitive Notifications on the same page, then go to Settings, tap Notifications, tap MoveMonkey and turn on Time Sensitive Notifications.

To have no reminders at all while a Focus is on (Sleep, for example), add MoveMonkey under that Focus’s Focus Filters and turn on Pause Sedentary Reminders (暂停久坐提醒).

Is an Apple Watch required?
No. The iPhone alone can remind you; with an Apple Watch on, standing is detected more accurately and reminders reach your wrist.

Is health data uploaded?
No. Everything is worked out on your devices; the server receives the reminder times worked out from it, not health data; see the Privacy Policy.
